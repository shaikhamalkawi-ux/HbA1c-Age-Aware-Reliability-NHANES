# Claim boundaries

- The central question is whether age partitioning improves point prediction and whether age matters for prediction reliability.
- Global residual and age-Mondrian conformal analyses are primary.
- Recovered original CQR is secondary. Later fixed-configuration CQR is post hoc exploratory only; later model objects must not be presented as the recovered original objects.
- Historical ANFIS is retained only as a structural/reproducibility case. Rule count is not biological evidence, and structural fidelity is not independent predictive validity.
- The reported hard-age OLS contrast crosses zero. Do not describe it as a stable accuracy improvement.
- Reduced subgroup coverage imbalance does not imply individual conditional coverage or nominal coverage in every age group.
- Temporal evaluations concern independent cross-sectional samples within NHANES, not longitudinal follow-up or validation in an independent health system.
- The primary temporal evaluations do not retrain or recalibrate on target outcomes.
- No laboratory-test replacement, treatment recommendation, or clinical adequacy claim is established by these materials.
- Reference numbers in this scaffold are expected audit targets. They are not computationally reproduced results.
